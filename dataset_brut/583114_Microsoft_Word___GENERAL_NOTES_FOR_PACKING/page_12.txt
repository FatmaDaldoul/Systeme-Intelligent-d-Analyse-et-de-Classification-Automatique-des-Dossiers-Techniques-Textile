PACKING:
Cartons and shipping marks
In order to prevent damaged cartons, we kindly ask you to protect do the following:
- logo cellotape (ok your company logo)
- wrap cartons on nylon – specially for sms and production shipment
- use plastic straps to close the cartons
- maximum weight of each carton shall not exceed the international weight limit (maximum 15/18 kgs)
- 60 cm (L) x 40 cm (W) x 40 cm (H) OR 60 cm (L) x 40 cm (W) x 50 cm (H) according to garment size
- The type of polybag to be used depends on the garment and is specified in the tech file and/or in this manual (previous pages)
